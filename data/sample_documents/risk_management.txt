Enterprise Risk Management Framework

Operational Risk
Operational risk can arise from failed processes, systems, people, or external events. Organizations should identify critical workflows and establish controls for high-impact processes.

Technology Risk
Technology systems should be monitored for availability, security, data quality, and reliability. Changes to production systems should be tested and documented before deployment.

Data Quality
Reliable analytics depend on complete, accurate, and consistent data. Data validation should include checks for missing records, duplicate records, invalid values, and inconsistent formats.

Risk Monitoring
Key risk indicators should be reviewed regularly. Material exceptions should be investigated, documented, and escalated when necessary.

Business Continuity
Critical systems should have recovery procedures and documented dependencies. Organizations should test recovery plans periodically to reduce disruption during unexpected failures.
